\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled ``Quantum Mechanical Theory of Ghosts'' for consideration as an Article in the \emph{Physics Teacher}. This work is intended as a pedagogical exercise that integrates several fundamental quantum-mechanical concepts—de Broglie wavelength, tunneling, Doppler shift, and Compton scattering—within a single illustrative, fictional narrative.

The manuscript is designed for instructors and undergraduate students and emphasizes order-of-magnitude reasoning, careful application of standard textbook formulas, and conceptual understanding. No claims are made regarding physical reality; the focus is strictly on teaching and demonstrating quantum concepts in an engaging context.

Thank you very much for your consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,\\[2ex]%
Babak Makkinejad, Ph.D.}
