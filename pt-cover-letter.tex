\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled ``Quantum Mechanical Theory of Ghosts'' for consideration in \emph{The Physics Teacher}. This manuscript presents a classroom-ready scenario that illustrates several fundamental quantum concepts—de Broglie wavelength, tunneling, Doppler shift, and Compton scattering—through a playful, engaging narrative. 

The work is designed to help high school and introductory college students visualize abstract ideas and can be readily incorporated into lectures or discussion activities. Its emphasis is on conceptual understanding and order-of-magnitude reasoning, making it immediately accessible for teaching purposes.

Thank you very much for your consideration. I look forward to your feedback.
